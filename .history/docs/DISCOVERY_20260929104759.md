# Discovery Notes: StayinCanada Client Portal

## Client & context

- StayinCanada Immigration: boutique, RCIC-led consultancy in Coquitlam, BC (since 2018, 1500+ families, serves clients in English, Arabic, Tamil, Tagalog)
- Project theme: AI-driven web solutions for small and medium-sized businesses
- Client idea chosen: Forms Filling & Client Workflows (internal). The client answers questions once, and the information flows into every required form, with RCIC review before anything reaches the client or IRCC
- Advisor: Amr Shehata (Founder, RCIC), ~1 hour/week, mid-September to end of November
- Stakeholders interviewed: Omar (Business/Marketing/Financial Partner), RCIC Partner handling operations

## Users

- Clients: prospective or retained immigrants/students, many with language barriers, who find long static forms intimidating
- Front desk/admin staff: verify forms and documents, create CRM and IRCC portal profiles
- RCIC: final legal review and IRCC submission (approval must stay with the RCIC)

## Pain points

- Clients submit incomplete Client Information Forms (missing family/sibling details, occupations, addresses)
- Data mismatches between the CIF, passport, resume and prior IRCC filings (spelling, dates)
- Uploaded documents are untranslated or exceed IRCC's 4 MB per-file limit
- Staff manually download and cross-check every CIF against Zoho Drive files
- Repetitive email/phone back-and-forth to fix minor errors frustrates clients and staff and delays submissions
- Staff manually key data into IRCC forms and create portal accounts, which causes typos and human error
- Clients have no visibility into progress, so they email and call for status updates
- Toolchain is fragmented (HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Zoho Drive, Zoho Click, email, WhatsApp) and is being consolidated into Zoho

## Proposed solution (scope)

Client portal that starts after the retainer is signed and paid:

- Client accounts and visa-type document checklists (shown only after retainer is paid)
- Smart step-by-step intake questionnaire replacing the static CIF, with auto-save so clients can leave and resume exactly where they left off
- Document upload with a 4 MB limit and AI checks for missing items and unapproved languages
- AI cross-check of questionnaire answers against uploaded documents (Omar: the most valuable feature)
- RCIC review/approval UI before anything is submitted
- Later: application status tracker, in-portal messaging

## Success metrics (KPIs)

Proposed targets, to confirm with the client:

- Fewer back-and-forth correction rounds per client than today
- 100% of uploads over 4 MB blocked before submission
- AI flags name/DOB/date mismatches between questionnaire and documents before staff review
- Meets or improves the 2-week turnaround from final documents to IRCC submission
- Fewer client status-update emails and calls
- Every AI-filled output is approved by an RCIC before it goes out

## Constraints

- Timeline: mid-September to end of November 2026, student team project with weekly Wednesday meetings (after 11:30 AM)
- Budget: to be confirmed with the client (assume minimal/no paid infrastructure until then)
- Technical: standalone web app with its own data model and auth, no Zoho integration for the MVP (see DECISIONS.md, 2026-09-29)
- Legal/guardrail: AI informs and verifies but never gives immigration advice, and the RCIC has final approval
- Scope risk: IRCC PDF auto-fill is a research spike, not a committed feature, until technical and legal feasibility is confirmed
- Data sensitivity: passports, dates of birth and family details require careful handling and access control

## Out of scope (for now)

- Lead generation chatbot (client's Idea 1)
- Zoho CRM/Drive integration
- Automating IRCC portal status checks (RCIC updates status manually for now)
