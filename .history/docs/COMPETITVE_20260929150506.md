## Competitive Matrix Table

| Competitor       | Category   | Target Audience                                               | Primary Focus / Strengths                                                                                                          | Key Weaknesses / Gaps                                                                                           |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Immigence OS     | Direct     | RCICs, immigration lawyers, consultants, legal firms          | All-in-one Canadian immigration OS; 400+ IRCC forms auto-fill; connected intake to case management; dynamic checklists; data reuse | Complex/broad feature set; extends beyond initial intake MVP needs; heavy setup for single-purpose workflows    |
| VisaFlo          | Direct     | RCICs, legal assistants, case coordinators, immigration firms | Case-specific workflows; smart error validation & timeline checks; structured document locking; direct contextual notes            | Overly broad scope; high learning curve; lacks step-by-step guidance for clients; obscures application progress |
| HubSpot          | Indirect   | Multi-departmental sales, marketing, and service teams        | Industry-leading centralized CRM records; ticket/pipeline tracking; powerful marketing and task automation                         | No native Canadian immigration knowledge; document checklists and IRCC workflows must be built from scratch     |
| Zoho             | Indirect   | General businesses seeking flexible CRM/ERP solutions         | Highly customizable (Zoho Forms, WorkDrive, Blueprint workflows); role-based permissions; strong conditional logic                 | Disconnected across separate apps (Forms, Drive, CRM); steep configuration burden; zero built-in IRCC logic     |
| Google Workspace | Substitute | General business users, small firms, solopreneurs             | Ultra-familiar tools (Forms, Drive, Sheets, Gmail); cloud-based real-time collaboration; low cost                                  | Fragmented workflow across multiple apps; manual file/folder maintenance; no built-in RCIC review or validation |
| Office 365       | Substitute | Corporate and small business teams                            | Established productivity suite (Forms, Excel, OneDrive, Outlook, Teams); flexible spreadsheet tracking                             | Highly manual tracking; fragmented user/client experience; zero immigration pathway intelligence                |
