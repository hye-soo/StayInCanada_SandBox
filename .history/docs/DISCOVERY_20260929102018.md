# Discovery Notes: Simple Calculator App

## Pain points

- Built-in OS calculators are slow to reach (unlock phone, find app)
- Most calculator apps are cluttered with ads or features nobody needs
- Students want something fast to reach during study sessions, in-browser

## Success metrics (KPIs)

- Time to first calculation under 2 seconds after page load
- Zero calculation errors on the four basic operations
- Works on both desktop and mobile browsers, no install required

## Constraints

- Timeline: 1 week, solo student project
- Budget: $0 — no paid APIs, no backend hosting
- Technical: static web app (Next.js), no database needed for v
